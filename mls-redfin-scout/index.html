🔖 Bookmarklet Installation & Guide

Our bookmarklet allows you to extract Matrix MLS property data and automatically enrich it with Redfin WalkScores and estimates straight from your browser without getting auto-blocked.

📍 Scrape to Scout

← Drag this button up to your browser bookmarks bar

How to use:

  1. Log in to your Matrix REColorado portal in another tab.
  2. Navigate to your Favorites tab or any search listing page.
  3. Click Scrape to Scout in your bookmarks bar (or press F12 on Matrix, open the Console tab, paste the snippet, and hit Enter).
  4. You will see a green popup: "Scraping Matrix MLS listings..." and "Scraped & Synced!"
  5. Return to this tab and refresh to see all your favorites updated!

🔬 Deep Scrape (full detail + all photos)

Walks every listing in your current search into its full detail view, one at a time. Every listing gets its notes re-checked on every run (in case your agent has replied), while the full photo gallery is only downloaded the first time a listing is fully captured — much slower on the first pass, fast on repeat runs.

🔬 Deep Scrape to Scout

← Drag this button up to your browser bookmarks bar

How to use:

  1. In Matrix, open one listing from your search into its full detail view (so the prev/next arrows are visible) — Deep Scrape starts from wherever you currently have expanded.
  2. Click Deep Scrape to Scout in your bookmarks bar (or paste the F12 console snippet).
  3. Leave the tab open — a progress popup appears for each listing (e.g. "12 of 57 — full scrape, 24 photos" or "13 of 57 — notes only") until the whole search has been walked.
  4. The final summary popup calls out any listings where a possible agent reply was detected in the notes, so you can double-check those.
  5. First run on a new search can take 15-30+ minutes; routine re-runs are much faster since previously-captured listings only get their notes re-checked.
