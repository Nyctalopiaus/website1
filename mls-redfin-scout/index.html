Scout Bookmarklet Installation & Guide

Our single-click bookmarklet extracts Matrix MLS property data, captures full photo galleries, ingests agent notes, and enriches listings straight from your browser.

Scrape to Scout

Drag this button up to your browser bookmarks bar

How to use:

  1. Log in to your Matrix REColorado portal in another tab.
  2. Go to your main search page (or any search list/favorites view).
  3. Click Scrape to Scout in your bookmarks bar (or run the F12 console snippet).
  4. The bookmarklet automatically performs an instant search list sync, enters the detail view, and steps through each listing — checking notes on every run and downloading full photo galleries for new listings.
  5. Progress popups will keep you updated (e.g. "12 of 57 — full scrape, 24 photos" or "13 of 57 — notes re-checked"). Routine re-runs are ultra-fast because cached photos are skipped automatically!
