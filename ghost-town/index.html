      How to hunt ghost towns

      WiGLE scores you for networks nobody has logged yet. Ghost Town finds neighborhoods full of networks nobody has seen in years. Most of those routers have since been replaced, and the replacements are new to WiGLE.

      Your key and your budget

      • Add your own WiGLE API key with the 🔑 button. It's encrypted with a PIN, stays in this browser and goes straight to WiGLE.
      • Every count or download page costs one query from your WiGLE daily allowance. New accounts get only a few a day; the allowance grows as you upload.
      • The budget bar shows today's usage. The first time WiGLE stops a scan, Ghost Town learns your daily limit and shows what's left.
      • Nothing is paid for twice. Counts are saved on one fixed grid, so overlapping areas reuse them, and they only refresh after 90 days when you ask.

      The efficient workflow

      1. Show your zip with cells of about 2 km, WiFi only, stale after 4 years. Each cell costs about 2 queries.
      2. Count cells. Each cell is finished before the next one starts, so even a small allowance gives you complete answers.
      3. Click the most promising cell and choose Split into 4 smaller cells. That spends a few queries on one spot instead of recounting the whole area.
      4. On your best small cell, choose Download devices to see individual networks. Filter for the stale ones and plan your route through them.

      Reading the map

      • Cells shade from blue (recently driven) to glowing pink (gone stale). Brighter cells hold more networks.
      • Gray cells have almost no networks on record. Check the streets underneath: a built-up gray cell has probably never been driven.
      • Rank by how many networks are stale, not the percentage: 400 stale out of 500 beats 9 out of 10.

      Tips

      • Only tick Bluetooth or Cell towers if you'll log them; each extra type doubles the cost.
      • Neighborhoods near colleges and apartment complexes turn over fastest, since tenants bring new routers every year or two.
      • Driving and uploading raises your WiGLE allowance, so each drive makes Ghost Town cheaper to use.
