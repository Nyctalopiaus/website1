🧭 Homeward Quick Start

Follow these steps to plan a home-viewing route, log field notes, and bring it all back for a report.

  1. 1) Set your starting point & stops Enter your home base address, then add homes you're interested in by pasting listing URLs (Redfin, Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com), using the 🔖 Bookmarklet, or importing a CSV file.
  2. 2) Optimize & build your tour route Click "Optimize & Build Tour Route" to auto-order every stop into the fastest driving loop, with live ETAs plotted on the map.
  3. 3) Take field notes at each stop Open a stop's 📝 Inspection Notebook to star-rate it, jot pros/cons and thoughts, attach a photo, and jump straight to its Redfin/Zillow listing or Street View.
  4. 4) Sync between your phone and PC Taking notes on your phone in the field? Tap 🔄 Sync Devices → Push to get a code, then Pull that code on your PC (or vice versa) to merge notes back in — no emailing files to yourself.
  5. 5) Export & report Generate a printable Executive Report, or export CSV/JSON any time to back up or share your findings.

Mostly private, browser-based storage Your tour is saved locally in this browser. It only leaves your device if you use Sync Devices, which stores it for 14 days under a random code that only you have.
