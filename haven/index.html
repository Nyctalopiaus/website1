Welcome to Stress-Free Budgeting

Budgeting isn't about restriction—it's about giving yourself permission to spend with total clarity. Haven helps you gently understand where your money goes, align with the beginner-friendly 50/30/20 framework, and build financial calm.

1. Your Monthly Income

Understand what you earn and what actually arrives in your bank account
Gross Income (On Paper)

What You Earn Before Deductions

Gross income is your headline salary or total hourly wages before anything is taken out. While it's what an offer letter states, it isn't what you can spend, because federal taxes, state taxes, Social Security, and health benefits are deducted first.

Net Income (Take-Home Pay)

The Real Money in Your Bank Account

Net income is the exact paycheck amount that gets directly deposited into your bank account. This is the true number you must budget with, so you never accidentally overspend on money that went to taxes or benefits.
