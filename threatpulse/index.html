THREATPULSE QUICK START GUIDE

Get up to speed in 4 simple steps with your distraction-free security advisory & technical intelligence dashboard.

  1. 1) Explore Live Feeds & CVEs: Browse normalized security advisories from CISA, BleepingComputer, Debian, Ubuntu, Cloudflare, SANS, and more.
  2. 2) Instant Filtering & Search: Click any category pill to filter feeds. Type in the search bar to find specific CVEs or topics.

THREATPULSE ENGINE CAPABILITIES

An executive-grade threat intelligence and advisory aggregation system built for speed, signal, and distraction-free monitoring.

  • Multi-Source Automated Ingestion: Aggregates RSS, Atom, and JSON feeds from official security advisories and homelab engineering feeds.
  • 2-Layer Deduplication Engine: Combines canonical SHA-256 link hashing with 85%+ fuzzy title matching over 24h windows to eliminate duplicate coverage.
  • Automatic CVE & Tag Extraction: Regex-based parsing identifies CVE tags and links them directly to the NIST National Vulnerability Database.
  • Interactive Column Toggle Buttons: Toggle category focus on the fly by clicking top filter pills or Kanban column header buttons.
  • Rotating KEV Threat Ticker: The Status & Highlights bar auto-rotates through actively-exploited CVEs every 6 seconds, with manual prev/next controls, so the top priority threats stay visible without extra clicks.
  • Automated DB Vacuum & Retention: Runs automated 30-day database retention pruning and SQLite WAL mode maintenance to maintain sub-50ms query speeds.
