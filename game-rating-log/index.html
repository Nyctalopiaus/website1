Handheld Gaming Log Features

A sleek, glassmorphic handheld game tracker built to log performance ratings, total play hours, and backlog statistics for your portable library.

  • Game Performance Ratings: Categorize titles by performance tiers (Great, Playable, or Poor).
  • Playtime Tracker: Log decimal hours played per game with instant total library hours calculation.
  • Library Analytics: Real-time overview cards showing Total Playtime, Game Count, and "Great Ratio" percentage.
  • Dynamic Sorting: Sort your collection by Title, Play Time, or Performance Rating with one-click ascending/descending toggles.
  • Card Management: Remove or update titles from your active list with instant UI refreshes.
🔒
100% Private & Local Storage Your entire game log and performance ratings live exclusively inside your browser's local storage. Nothing is stored on any server.
